# How to Add Photos

Place your sister's wedding photos in this folder (`assets/images/`):

1. **story-1.jpg**: First meeting / early memories photo (recommended aspect ratio: 4:3 or square).
2. **story-2.jpg**: Growing together / travel / special date photo.
3. **story-3.jpg**: Proposal / engagement photo.
4. **venue.jpg**: Photo of the wedding venue / ballroom.
5. **og-preview.jpg**: The preview picture shown when you send the website link on WhatsApp, Viber, or Facebook. (Recommended: 1200x630px).

> **Tip:** You can compress your photos for free at https://tinypng.com before adding them to ensure the website loads lightning-fast on mobile phones!
