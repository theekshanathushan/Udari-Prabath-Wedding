AUSPICIOUS TIMINGS

Ceremony Itinerary

Please join us early to take part in every traditional ritual and blessing.

🌹
🥁
08:30 AM

Arrival & Magul Bera Welcome

Traditional drum greetings and welcome refreshments as guests are seated in the Grand Ballroom.

🏛️
⭐ AUSPICIOUS NEKATHA ⭐
09:14 AM

The Sacred Poruwa Ceremony

Ascending the Poruwa, chanting of Jayamangala Gatha, exchange of rings, tying of the Nuul, and breaking of the traditional coconut.

🪔
10:15 AM

Lighting the Traditional Oil Lamp

Lighting of the Pana Pahan symbolizing prosperity, followed by wedding cake cutting and toasts.

🥂
12:30 PM

Wedding Banquet Feast

A festive buffet of Sri Lankan delicacies and international cuisine served with heartfelt wishes.

💃
02:00 PM Onwards

Music, Photographs & Farewell

Celebratory dancing, photographs with the newlyweds, and warm goodbyes as the celebration concludes.
